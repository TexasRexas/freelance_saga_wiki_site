Cultures of Alterra

Each culture card opens a lore entry written in the style of the Alterra culture documents. These entries record ancestry, history, physicality, naming conventions, and archetypes useful for storytelling or tabletop play.

Dwarves

Mountain-born inventors, crystal crafters, clan traditionalists, and caretakers of songs nearly lost to the Cataclysm.

Orcs

Wasteland survivors, selective bloodlines, border guardians, brewers, mercenaries, and inheritors of the Orc Wars.

Halflings

Sailors, cartographers, record keepers, airship builders, and guardians of the ancient world’s memory.

Humans

Fragile by comparison, relentless by necessity, and foundational to the Federation’s survival.

Ormyri

Dragon-descended people of Highwind, shaped by elemental ancestry, brood legacy, and survival through alliance.

Elves

Long-lived magical architects of the old world, burdened by the Cataclysm and divided over redemption or restoration.

Giants

Mountain custodians, storm-listeners, shattered clans, and patient keepers of the world’s oldest stones.

Merfolk

Coral city dwellers, storm-hidden pacifists, memory singers, and guardians of the western archipelagos.

Gnomes

Littlefoot innovators, restless experimenters, magitech pioneers, and specialists in making the future explode less.
