Guilds and Progression

The guild system turns archetypes into social institutions. Each guild is both a character path and a piece of the Federation's culture.

Church Orders

Clerics serve through divine invocation, religious service, holy scholarship, public inspiration, and martial enforcement.

Arena Guilds

Fighters progress through gladiatorial circuits, combat schools, public celebrity, and sanctioned battlefield service.

Arcane Societies

Wizards study Dominus Primers, Arcanum Scholastica, Loci, spell seals, research ethics, and experimental magic.

Grand Masques

Rogues hide espionage, intrigue, performance, and covert operations beneath theater troupes and public spectacle.

Concordance Movement

Sorcerers organize around natural magic, civil rights, community safety, and the dignity of those born with innate mana.

Freelancer Association

Adventurers, specialists, and contract workers accept jobs across Union City, the Riftlands, and beyond.
