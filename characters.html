Characters

The main party represents the future of Alterra: cultural blending, innovation, community care, responsibility, historical truth, and chosen connection.

Clayton Silverhall

Champion of Avmir, half Dwarf and half Orc, healer, alchemist, barista, and emotional center of the party.

Miranda Greene

Secret natural mage, adventurer, artificer, and catalyst for chaos hiding inside elite wizard society.

A'eryn Basch

Exiled elven Blade Magus and ferrofluid weapon innovator challenging tradition through technology.

Ashwin Swift

Graffiti sigil healer, found-family seeker, and street-level public servant.

River Cobalt

Veteran Ormyri gladiator, mentor, spokesperson, and hidden well of grief.

Orion Riverrunner

Halfling infiltrator, historian, survivor, and truth-bearer of forgotten conspiracies.
