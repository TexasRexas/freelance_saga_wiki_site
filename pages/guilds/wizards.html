Arcane Societies

Academic research societies where spellcraft, seal theory, magical ethics, and institutional prestige shape wizard life.

Overview

The Arcane Societies of the Federation govern the education, experimentation, and preservation of magical knowledge. Wizards are expected to understand the principles beneath spellcasting rather than merely produce magical effects. Their craft centers on three components: the Dominus Primer, which evokes a domain; the Arcanum Scholastica, which assigns a school of magic; and the Loci, which determines range.

Traditionally, wizards traced seals in the air with wands. Modern casters often carry spell cards inscribed with complete or partial seals, while more inventive wizards experiment with tattoos, instruments, weapons, jewelry, or other unconventional mediums.

Culture

Wizard society is built around study, publication, experimentation, and reputation. Prestigious institutions such as Primeri Magia create enormous opportunities for those who fit their expectations, but they can also reinforce elitism, bloodline politics, and suspicion of natural mages. To many trained wizards, magic without formal education is dangerous; to many outsiders, wizard caution looks like gatekeeping.

The societies are not united in temperament. Some scholars seek knowledge for its own sake, while Magisters want practical inventions and Arbiters worry about containment. Public-facing Luminaries shape the image of arcane life, turning discoveries into political and cultural influence.

Progression

Progression in the Arcane Societies depends on proof of mastery. Scholars rise through research and thesis work. Magisters advance through patents, magical devices, ritual arrays, and field applications. Luminaries rise through reputation and public influence, while Arbiters earn rank by investigating dangerous magic and enforcing arcane regulation.

Classifications

Scholars

Scholars are students, librarians, assistants, and junior researchers responsible for preserving magical theory and studying the interaction between Primers, Arcanum, and Loci.

Ranks: Apprentice, Researcher, Lecturer, Professor, Dean

Magisters

Magisters are practical arcane specialists who create spell cards, magical devices, ritual arrays, and new seal combinations.

Ranks: Scribe, Artificer, Thaumaturge, Archmagister, High Curator

Luminaries

Luminaries are celebrated public intellectuals whose discoveries reshape society and whose reputations make them representatives of magical progress.

Ranks: Fellow, Distinguished Fellow, Eminent Scholar, Grand Luminary, Chancellor

Arbiters

Arbiters regulate arcane society by investigating dangerous experiments, identifying unstable seals, and containing magical threats.

Ranks: Examiner, Inspector, Adjudicator, High Arbiter, Grand Inquisitor
