Arena Guilds

Gladiatorial institutions where combat is profession, spectacle, public identity, and sanctioned violence.

Overview

The martial guilds of the Federation are most strongly associated with the Grand Arenas, institutions that function as military academies, entertainment venues, training schools, and political stages. Fighters of every background come to the arenas to prove themselves, earn coin, develop technique, and build reputations that may carry them into mercenary work or official Federation service.

Though every arena has its own traditions, preferred weapons, and local celebrities, the overall hierarchy is widely recognized. Members are sorted into Brawlers, Duelists, Icons, and Reavers according to whether their work centers on physical competition, technical mastery, public fame, or battlefield application.

Culture

Arena culture values competence, courage, and spectacle. A victory without style may earn respect, but a victory that moves the crowd can build a career. This makes the Arena Guilds places where martial excellence and performance constantly overlap. A fighter is not only judged by whether they survive, but by the story spectators can tell afterward.

Despite the public entertainment surrounding them, the arenas are not frivolous. Federation cities rely on trained fighters as guards, proctors, bodyguards, contract soldiers, and Riftland specialists. Arena graduates frequently become Freelancers, while retired champions often become instructors or political symbols.

Progression

Progression through the Arena Guilds is measured through victories, contracts, instruction, fame, or sanctioned service. A Brawler’s rise may begin in the pits, while a Duelist earns rank by developing techniques and defeating notable opponents. Icons rise by public acclaim, and Reavers advance through military contracts, dangerous missions, and wartime accomplishments.

Classifications

Brawlers

Brawlers are frontline competitors, guards, caravan escorts, and exhibition combatants. They are the laboring body of the arenas and often begin with raw endurance before developing specialized styles.

Ranks: Pitborn, Chainfighter, Blooded, Arena Veteran, Coliseum Master

Duelists

Duelists are technical combat specialists who treat battle as theory, discipline, and art. They are instructors, tacticians, judges, and masters of specialized forms.

Ranks: Cadet, Bladescholar, Tactician, Warmaster, Grand Marshal

Icons

Icons are fighters who become public legends. They represent regional pride, guild values, and the power of spectacle to shape culture.

Ranks: Prospect, Headliner, Champion, Grand Champion, Living Legend

Reavers

Reavers are battlefield specialists trained for war rather than show. They serve as mercenaries, bounty hunters, bodyguards, and officers under arena patronage.

Ranks: Hound, Breaker, Ravager, Dreadblade, War Reaver
