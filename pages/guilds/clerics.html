Church Orders

The religious guilds of the Federation, organized around divine service, invocations, holy scholarship, public example, and sacred authority.

Overview

The churches of the Federation organize their members through a shared guild structure regardless of the deity being worshiped. Whether one serves Avmir, Lord of the Untamed Wilds, Zinos, Paragon of the Pack, or another divine power, the path through the organization follows a familiar pattern. Members are divided into Acolytes, Theurges, Champions, and Wardens, each of which reflects a different way of serving the divine.

Divine magic is not altered in the same fashion as arcane or natural magic. Invocations are known prayers of power granted through a deity’s authority, and they perform consistently each time they are called. Because the power flows from deity to conduit, the churches have developed strong hierarchies determining who may be trusted with greater divine force.

Culture

Church Orders are not merely places of worship. They are community centers, healing houses, archives, courts, sanctuaries, and sometimes military powers. Each church interprets service through the nature of its deity, so a temple of Avmir may value cultivation, wilderness care, and communal resilience, while a temple of Zinos may organize itself around healing, pack loyalty, and protection of the vulnerable.

This structure makes the churches one of the most visible forms of authority in ordinary Federation life. Their influence can be comforting or oppressive depending on the deity, the local leadership, and the community they serve. The same organization that feeds refugees and heals the sick may also pass judgment on heresy or enforce divine law.

Progression

Progression through a Church Order depends on the chosen classification. Acolytes advance through service to the church, community works, sermons, or acts of reverence. Theurges advance by creating relics, performing rituals, and developing spells or sacred tools in service to a deity. Champions rise through public perception and divine example, while Wardens are promoted through trials, enforcement, and defense of divine order.

Classifications

Acolytes

Acolytes channel divinity through prayers and religious tools. They form the workforce and lay body of most churches, serving as priests, healers, caretakers, teachers, and ritual assistants.

Ranks: Deacon, Priest, Bishop, Archbishop, Cardinal

Theurges

Theurges use arcane magic in service to a deity. They are researchers, ritualists, relic creators, and holy inventors who explore how mortal spellcraft can support divine purpose.

Ranks: Evangelist, Esoteric, Savant, Seneschal, Vizier

Champions

Champions are individuals blessed by providence or chosen for exemplifying the deepest beliefs of a church. They are the public face of divine ideals and often carry immense symbolic weight.

Ranks: Langrave, Margrave, Graffen, Marchionne, Baron

Wardens

Wardens are the martial arm of the churches, serving as judges, protectors, investigators, and executioners when matters of divine authority demand force.

Ranks: Page, Squire, Knight Errant, Knight Major, Knight Commander
