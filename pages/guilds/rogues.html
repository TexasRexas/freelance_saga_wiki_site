Grand Masques

Theater troupes, performance houses, carnivals, spies, thieves, and information brokers hidden beneath the glamour of the stage.

Overview

The Grand Masques are the most famous performance networks in the Federation and the least publicly understood of the major guilds. On the surface they are theater troupes, traveling carnivals, dance companies, poets, musicians, stagehands, costumers, and celebrity performers. Beneath the lights and applause exists a hidden culture of spies, thieves, con artists, burglars, and information brokers.

Rogues in Alterra do not simply skulk in alleys. They understand that every society is a stage and every person performs. The Grand Masques teach members how to control what others see, when to disappear, and how to turn attention itself into a weapon.

Culture

Masque culture values adaptability, presentation, secrecy, and social reading. A successful member knows how to enter a room as exactly the person that room expects. Some use this talent for art, some for politics, and others for crime. The guild’s public respectability protects its covert work, while covert information often protects its performers from exploitation by nobles, corporations, and rival powers.

Because the Masques move across cities and districts, they often know stories before officials do. A playwright may hear rumors in a noble salon, a stagehand may map secret passages while building sets, and a dancer may learn which politician fears which scandal. Their power is soft until it is not.

Progression

Progression through the Grand Masques depends on successful performances, profitable operations, influence gained, secrets acquired, and missions completed without exposure. Public acclaim matters for Virtuosos, while Shadows advance through work few people ever learn happened.

Classifications

Players

Players are the backbone of the Masques: actors, stagehands, pickpockets, messengers, and street performers who learn versatility before specialization.

Ranks: Extra, Featured Player, Lead Performer, Star Performer, Master of the Masque

Gallants

Gallants specialize in social manipulation, courtly infiltration, seduction, diplomacy, confidence schemes, and political spying.

Ranks: Courtier, Confidant, Envoy, Silver Tongue, Grand Gallant

Virtuosos

Virtuosos are celebrated artistic masters whose performances can influence culture, preserve identity, or ignite resistance.

Ranks: Trouper, Maestro, Virtuoso, Grand Virtuoso, Immortal Muse

Shadows

Shadows are covert operatives trained beneath the guise of performance. Assassins, burglars, blackmailers, and spies walk this path.

Ranks: Stagehand, Whisper, Phantom, Veilmaster, Grand Shadow
