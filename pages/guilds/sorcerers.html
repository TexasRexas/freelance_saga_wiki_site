Concordance Movement

A civil rights organization protecting natural mages, challenging magical discrimination, and giving sorcerers a political voice.

Overview

The Concordance Movement organizes Sorcerers not through academies or bloodline houses, but through advocacy, mutual aid, and community defense. Sorcerers, also called natural mages, manipulate mana directly through hand signs and bodily flow patterns rather than external seals. Their gestures evoke the same basic functions as a wizard’s Primer, Arcanum, and Loci, but the magic moves through the body instead of being written outside it.

Natural magic is powerful, flexible, and unpredictable. Most Sorcerers show talent in only one or two schools of spellcasting, but under pressure they can improvise in ways trained wizards find difficult to anticipate. This has made them both valuable and feared.

Culture

The Concordance began as a coalition against persecution and magical discrimination. In elite magical spaces, natural mages are often treated as dangerous, improper, or embarrassing to families with prestigious wizard bloodlines. The Movement exists to insist that innate magic is not a defect and that safety comes from support rather than suppression.

Concordance chapters provide mentorship, legal aid, shelter, political advocacy, magical control training, and protection from hostile institutions. Their work is especially important for young mages whose powers awaken before they have the language or social protection to understand what is happening.

Progression

Progression through the Concordance is based on self-mastery, community involvement, political success, cultural influence, and protection of vulnerable Sorcerers. The organization values survival as much as achievement, understanding that for many natural mages the first victory is simply being able to live openly.

Classifications

Kindled

Kindled are newly awakened or developing Sorcerers learning to control innate gifts while navigating the danger of public exposure.

Ranks: Awakened, Kindled, Emberborn, Flamebearer, Beacon

Advocates

Advocates focus on legal reform, negotiation, organizing, and political defense of Sorcerer rights.

Ranks: Organizer, Representative, Advocate, Tribune, High Speaker

Heralds

Heralds are inspirational public figures who use magic, speech, art, and reputation to reshape public opinion.

Ranks: Voice, Orator, Herald, Luminary, Icon of Concordance

Guardians

Guardians protect Sorcerer communities from persecution, corruption, and violence through intervention, escort work, and resistance when necessary.

Ranks: Shieldbearer, Warden, Protector, Sentinel, Concord Paladin
