Freelancer Association

The contract network that sends adventurers, specialists, guides, guards, and problem-solvers wherever the Federation needs them most.

Overview

The Freelancer Association is one of the most visible institutions in modern Alterra, existing at the crossroads of commerce, public service, exploration, and survival. Where the major guilds represent specialized paths, the Association gathers people capable of taking contracts across disciplines. A Freelancer might be a healer, scout, fighter, engineer, scholar, monster hunter, courier, or all of these things depending on the work available.

The Association became especially important because of the Riftlands. No centralized military force could safely handle every rescue, recovery mission, monster incursion, relic hunt, or escort contract. Freelancers filled that gap by creating a flexible labor structure where independent parties could accept dangerous work with official oversight.

Culture

Freelancer culture is practical, varied, and built on reputation. A party’s name can open doors or close them. Successful Freelancers are valued not only for combat skill, but for judgment, reliability, adaptability, and the ability to return alive with the job completed.

To ordinary citizens, Freelancers are both heroes and warning signs. Their arrival can mean help is coming, but it can also mean something has gone wrong enough that local authorities cannot handle it. Many young people romanticize the lifestyle, while veterans know how quickly the work can destroy the unprepared.

Progression

The Association’s progression is less ceremonial than the other guilds and more directly tied to completed contracts, peer evaluation, sponsorship, and survival record. Entrance exams and proctors exist to keep the reckless from dying too quickly, though plenty of candidates still underestimate the cost of the work.

Classifications

Prospects

Prospects are applicants, trainees, and low-rank contract workers still proving they can operate safely and reliably.

Ranks: Applicant, Initiate, Junior Freelancer

Specialists

Specialists are recognized for a particular skill set such as healing, scouting, engineering, combat, research, or negotiation.

Ranks: Associate, Specialist, Senior Specialist

Party Leads

Party Leads coordinate teams in the field, negotiate contracts, and make decisions when plans collapse.

Ranks: Lead, Veteran Lead, Contract Captain

Guild Exemplars

Exemplars are famous Freelancers whose names carry political, cultural, or instructional weight within the Association.

Ranks: Proctor, Guild Representative, Living Contract
