Orcs

Wasteland survivors, selective bloodlines, border guardians, brewers, mercenaries, and inheritors of the Orc Wars.

History

The ancestral lands of the Orcs were harsh even before the Cataclysm. Much of their northern territory was infertile wasteland, forcing Orc communities to innovate in agriculture until they could tame portions of the uninhabited North. Most Orcs lived in tight-knit tribes positioned near neighboring kingdoms for trade and, later, war.

Orcish fruits adapted to the wastes and weather-resistant textiles became valuable trade goods. As Orcs interacted with neighboring peoples, they began selective breeding with other races in an attempt to encourage beneficial mutations. Over generations this produced tribes that varied wildly from one another.

By the time of the Endless War, three major Orc tribes had amassed most of the power. Green Orcs of the mountainous north were the largest and strongest. Blue Orcs near the Lycan forests developed animalistic traits such as horns, tails, and boar-like tusks. Red Orcs near the Ormyri domain possessed draconic traits such as scales and wings. Together their leaders formed a War Council that made Orcs terrifying in battle, though infighting and competition prevented them from conquering the world.

When the Cataclysm changed Alterra, many Orc tribes were wiped out, leaving the Red, Green, and Blue tribes as the primary survivors. Some scholars claim Gnomes are descended from Orcs who bred with Halflings, though this has never been proven.

Culture

After the Cataclysm, the Orc War Council could not agree on how to respond to the new Federation. The Red Orc leader wanted to wage war while other nations were weak. The Blue Orcs wanted to join the Federation early and seize influence from within. The Green Orcs wanted peace and separation. When Nikhil Suneater of the Red Orcs slew the other leaders in ritual combat and declared himself King of the Orcs, the long campaign of resistance known as the Orc Wars began.

The Orc Wars isolated the Orcs from the resources and developments enjoyed by the Federation. By the time they entered Federation society, they had fallen behind the other races in infrastructure and technology. Their long martial history led many Orcs to become a primary fighting force against creatures of the Rift, building rough cities around the Riftlands.

Green Orcs often live on the fringes of civilization as guardians of the natural world, hunters, or mercenaries. Blue Orcs are more entwined with Federation cities, using powerful Orcish alcohol and secret brewing techniques to gain wealth and influence. Red Orcs mostly inhabit the dangerous ring of border cities around the Riftlands, where their love of risk has been tempered into practical expertise.

Naming Conventions

Across the surviving tribes, Orc naming conventions remain the same. At birth, an Orc receives a name describing what their parents hope for them in the Orc language. Orcs are not considered adults until they accomplish something worthy of a deed-name. Feminine names include Aanya, Kriti, Priya, and Saanvi. Masculine names include Vivaan, Kabir, Kiaan, and Aarav. Deed-names include Stonecleaver, Giantfeller, Stormchaser, and Shadowstalker.

Physicality

Green Orcs are massive and strong, with adults commonly towering over members of other races. Blue Orcs display the most visible variation because of Lycan ancestry, often showing horns, protruding tusks, tails, or other animalistic traits. Red Orcs carry draconic features from old proximity to Ormyri territory, including scales, wing-like growths, hardened skin, and heat-resistant bodies.

Orc bodies reflect generations of adaptation. Whether shaped for wasteland survival, border defense, or city politics, most Orcs possess impressive stamina and a physical presence that still causes suspicion among people who remember the Orc Wars.

Archetypes

Nemophilists are Green Orcs at one with the natural world. Survival, natural knowledge, animal kinship, and discipline allow them to live in harmony with untamed lands.

Hospitaliers are Blue Orcs who master the social battlefield of Federation cities. Charm, Diplomacy, Observation, and Empathy make them suited for situations where words work as well as fists.

Madcaps are Red Orc daredevils from the Riftland border cities. Weapons training, Athletics, Vigor, and Intimidation help them face down horrors where caution often fails.

Condottieri are Orc mercenaries who make a life protecting those with coin to hire them. Defense, Discipline, Willpower, and Vigor prepare them for uncertain work.

Ascetics are Orcs who remained in the Northern Wastes after the Harmony Accords. Survival, Willpower, Lore, and Spiritual Knowledge guide lives focused on endurance, simplicity, and the land itself.
