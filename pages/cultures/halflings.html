Halflings

Sailors, cartographers, record keepers, airship builders, and guardians of the ancient world’s memory.

History

There was once a time when the rivers and coasts of Alterra were littered with Halfling ships. Originating from a floating barge in the Western seas that functioned as their capital, Halfling fleets traveled the world gathering pieces of everyone they encountered. Each ship usually consisted of a single family unit operating as crew, and every vessel carried detailed ledgers of what they saw, collected, ate, and traded.

Over time, Halflings became the go-to traders for many independent kingdoms. Seeking to expand their knowledge further, the Armada sent ships west into unmapped ocean. Many did not return, and those that did told of a great storm blocking the waters. Conquering this storm became an obsession of the Halfling Armada.

When the first ships finally crossed the barrier, they found a tropical archipelago and coral cities belonging to the Merfolk. The Merfolk had raised the storm to close themselves off from the world. Though wary, they welcomed the Halflings and offered trade goods and passage in exchange for protection should the other races seek to plunder their paradise.

Knowledge of this new land helped ignite expansionist ambition in other nations, and some scholars say this moment sparked the Endless War. The Halfling Armada defended the western waters and their Merfolk allies until the Cataclysm made the seas unsafe. Monsters rose from the depths and attacked ships indiscriminately, forcing Halflings ashore. There they found kinship with the Gnomes of the Western plains and formed the Littlefoot Coalition.

Culture

Since Unification, many Halflings have abandoned the waters and moved into large cities. The ledgers once kept by nomadic barges became essential to preserving the ancient world. Halfling artisans used old accounts to recreate art and architecture thought lost to the Rift, while scholars turned travel records into books for future generations.

Shipwrights adapted their carpentry to bridges and eventually airships, allowing Halflings to sail the skies as their ancestors sailed the waters. Old sailors used star charts and maps to locate ruins and places of power. Few people in the modern world know the past as well as Halflings, and many serve as professors, curators, restoration specialists, and members of the Swiftfoot Brotherhood.

Though much of Halfling culture focuses on preserving what was lost, magitech has integrated surprisingly well. Airships in particular have become beloved symbols of return, offering a way for Halflings to reclaim the motion and freedom of their seafaring ancestors.

Naming Conventions

Halfling names are made of a given name usually connected to space or celestial bodies, reflecting the importance of stars and moon to ancient navigation, and a family name often connected to nautical life. Feminine names include Astra, Nova, Aurora, and Stella. Masculine names include Cosmo, Sunny, Aster, and Orion. Family names include Riverrunner, Seabrook, Bareboat, and Porter.

Physicality

Halflings are small and nimble, with adult males reaching an upper range of about five feet and adult females often reaching about four feet nine inches. Their frames are lithe and wiry, well suited for life aboard ships crowded with extended family.

Despite their small build, Halflings can be deceptively strong from generations of rowing and hoisting sails. Prolonged time at sea gave many tough, almost leathery skin, though this trait is becoming less common. Semi-webbed toes, large feet, and excellent lung capacity reflect their semi-aquatic past.

Archetypes

Historians use ancestral ledgers to study ancient history, culture, and linguistics. Lore, Observation, Crafts, and Artifice help them preserve what others forget.

Suppliers embrace the mercantile traditions of old and carry goods between Federation cities. Appraisal, Diplomacy, Charm, and Culture are essential tools of their trade.

Trailblazers cannot ignore the call of exploration. Vehicles, Athletics, Survival, and Charm help them uncover forgotten routes, ruins, and stories.
