Giants

Mountain custodians, storm-listeners, shattered clans, and patient keepers of the world’s oldest stones.

History

The Giants once ruled the highest peaks of Alterra long before modern civilization. Massive even by today’s standards, ancient Giant clans believed themselves chosen custodians of the world’s natural order. Unlike Dragons, who consumed elemental wells, Giants worshipped them.

The earliest Giant civilizations formed around sacred mountain ranges where they believed the voices of the world could be heard through stone, thunder, and shifting earth. Their oral histories claim the Giants were shaped from the bones of Alterra itself.

During the Endless War, Giant territories became targets for their immense resources and strategic elevation. Initially isolationist, Giants eventually retaliated against Dwarven and Elven expansion into their sacred mountains. These conflicts devastated both sides.

Though individually powerful, Giants always had small populations and recovered slowly from war. The Cataclysm shattered their civilization further as Rift creatures infested mountain ranges and awakened horrors beneath the earth. Many clans fractured into wandering tribes, and only scattered strongholds remain.

Culture

Giant culture is among the most spiritually oriented in Alterra. Giants believe the world itself is alive and that every mountain, river, and storm carries memory. Patience and endurance are sacred virtues. Rushing is immaturity, wastefulness is sacrilege, and greed is a sickness of the spirit.

Many modern Giants view Federation society as loud, impatient, and disconnected from the natural world. Even so, younger Giants increasingly travel beyond ancestral territories seeking purpose in a changing era. Some become construction workers, transport specialists, Rift defenders, wilderness guides, or spiritual teachers.

Because of their immense size and strength, Giants often struggle to integrate into dense cities. Federation architecture rarely accommodates them comfortably outside specialized districts, and this reinforces a sense that they are respected as symbols but not always welcomed as citizens.

Naming Conventions

Giant names are typically short, heavy sounding, and tied to natural phenomena or geological features. Feminine names include Hilda, Sigrun, Yrsa, and Thora. Masculine names include Bjorn, Ragnar, Sten, and Torvald. Clan names include Stormpeak, Ironroot, Frostvein, and Stonewake.

Physicality

Giants are the largest humanoid people of Alterra, towering over most other races with bodies built for endurance and raw strength. Their features often appear weathered, with thick skin, heavy bones, and eyes that seem accustomed to looking across great distances.

Regional clans show variation based on the mountains, tundras, or highlands where they settled. Some possess stone-gray undertones, frost-pale hair, or skin marked by rough textures that outsiders compare to bark, granite, or storm-scarred cliff faces.

Archetypes

Earthshapers are masters of stonework and terrain manipulation, relying on Vigor, Survival, Crafting, and Discipline.

Runekeepers preserve Giant oral traditions and spiritual memory through Lore, Ritualism, Observation, and Culture.

Avalanches are massive frontline combatants who rely on Heavy Weapons, Defense, Vigor, and Intimidation.

Wayfarers guide caravans and explorers through dangerous regions using Survival, Vehicles, Observation, and Leadership.
