Merfolk

Coral city dwellers, storm-hidden pacifists, memory singers, and guardians of the western archipelagos.

History

For much of recorded history, the Merfolk lived completely separated from the rest of Alterra behind a massive magical storm barrier raised by their shamans. Hidden beyond the western seas, their archipelagos flourished in peace while the surface world descended into conflict.

This isolation ended when Halfling explorers pierced the storm and discovered the coral cities of the Merfolk. Recognizing the danger posed by expansionist kingdoms, the Merfolk formed an alliance with the Halfling Armada. They offered trade and passage in exchange for protection should other races attempt to plunder their paradise.

Knowledge of western resources helped intensify expansionist ambition among the old kingdoms. The Merfolk remained largely defensive throughout the conflict, relying on oceanic magic and Halfling naval superiority to preserve sovereignty.

After the Cataclysm, the seas grew dangerous. Riftspawn leviathans, unstable storms, and corrupted currents devastated trade routes and isolated settlements. Portions of Merfolk society eventually integrated with the Federation out of necessity, though many still mourn the loss of their hidden peace.

Culture

Merfolk culture revolves around harmony, memory, and emotional interconnectedness. Unlike many surface peoples, Merfolk place enormous value on emotional honesty and communal wellbeing. They often find surface politics exhausting because so much of it depends on concealing feeling rather than understanding it.

Music shapes daily life. Merfolk songs preserve history, guide navigation, perform ritual, comfort grief, and mark communal decisions. A song may function as a map, a family record, a prayer, or a warning depending on how it is performed.

Because their civilization developed in relative peace, outsiders often mistake Merfolk for naive. In truth they are deeply perceptive emotionally and socially. Their difficulty is not ignorance of conflict, but distaste for the cruelty surface races often accept as normal.

Naming Conventions

Merfolk names are flowing and lyrical, often inspired by tides, stars, weather, and sea life. Feminine names include Nerina, Selene, Marina, and Thalassa. Masculine names include Caspian, Nereus, Kael, and Maris. Family names include Coralheart, Wavecaller, Deepcurrent, and Tidewhisper.

Physicality

Merfolk possess semi-aquatic bodies adapted to long periods in water and coastal environments. Their features vary by archipelago, but many have smooth skin, fin-like accents, webbed fingers or toes, and excellent breath control. Some can move comfortably on land for extended periods, while others require water access or specialized accommodations.

Hair, skin, and eye coloration often reflect reef environments, deep sea tones, or storm-lit waters. Surface dwellers sometimes describe Merfolk beauty as unsettling because it follows the logic of ocean life rather than land-bound expectations.

Archetypes

Tidesingers use music and emotional magic through Performance, Empathy, Restoration, and Culture.

Wavewardens defend coastal settlements with Polearms, Athletics, Survival, and Defense.

Seekers explore and navigate through Vehicles, Observation, Lore, and Survival.

Reefshapers blend water magic with architecture and artistry through Crafting, Arcana, and Artifice.
