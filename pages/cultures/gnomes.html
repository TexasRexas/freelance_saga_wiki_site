Gnomes

Littlefoot innovators, restless experimenters, magitech pioneers, and specialists in making the future explode less.

History

The origins of the Gnomes remain heavily debated. Some scholars believe they descend from Halfling-Orc bloodlines, while others insist they emerged independently in the western plains after the Cataclysm. Whatever the truth, Gnomes became close allies of Halflings during the collapse of the old world.

Together with Halflings, Gnomes formed the Littlefoot Coalition to resist larger and more aggressive powers during the Federation’s early years. While Halflings focused on preserving the past through ledgers and memory, Gnomes became obsessed with building the future.

Their curiosity and adaptability made them pioneers of magitech engineering. Many modern Federation conveniences exist because some Gnome looked at an exhausting task and decided it would be better if the device exploded less often.

Culture

Gnomes are energetic, intensely curious, and culturally allergic to stagnation. Where Dwarves perfect, Gnomes iterate. Failure is not considered shameful; refusing to try is. This makes them some of the most innovative minds in Alterra and some of the most dangerous roommates imaginable.

Gnome communities often combine laboratories, workshops, marketplaces, and social clubs until the difference between work and recreation becomes unclear. Their culture values enthusiasm and creativity far more than hierarchy. A respected elder is not necessarily the oldest person in the room, but the one who still asks the most interesting questions.

Federation society relies heavily on Gnome innovation, though not everyone is comfortable admitting it. Gnomes are often invited into projects after the respectable engineers have failed and someone desperate finally says, quietly, that perhaps the mad little gearbox people had a point.

Naming Conventions

Gnome names are playful, inventive, and highly individualized. Families often accept nicknames, workshop names, and self-chosen titles as easily as birth names. Feminine names include Pippa, Tink, Lumi, and Wren. Masculine names include Nix, Pip, Fizz, and Otto. Family names include Cogturner, Brightspark, Wobblegear, and Quickwire.

Physicality

Gnomes are small, quick, and physically expressive, often built with wiry frames suited for cramped workshops and rapid movement through chaotic environments. Their hands are famously nimble, and many develop calluses, burn marks, ink stains, or tiny scars from years of experimentation.

Because their origins are disputed, Gnomes vary more than outsiders expect. Some display Halfling-like proportions, others possess sharper Orcish features in miniature, and still others show traits difficult to place at all. This ambiguity has become part of Gnome identity rather than a problem to solve.

Archetypes

Tinkerers are inventors and engineers who use Artifice, Crafting, Research, and Observation to build solutions others would never attempt.

Scamps are fast-talking opportunists who rely on Sleight of Hand, Charm, Athletics, and Deception.

Sparkcasters are experimental magitech spell users who blend Arcana, Artifice, and Spellcraft.

Pathfinders are curious explorers who use Survival, Vehicles, Observation, and Appraisal to discover new routes and opportunities.
